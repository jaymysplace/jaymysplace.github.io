Saw this suggested on one of Kathleen Spracklen's videos [1]: print out a picture of the book's cover and include it on the bibcard!

Is it a good idea? You'll remember the book by the cover, and your notebox is there to help you remember. It'd be easier to find the bibcard that you're looking for too. I know they're already in alphabetical order but I think you'll spot the one you're looking for instantly when you see the cover as you're flicking through.

On the other hand, I do wonder if it's added fluff, like making fancy headers and cover pages in a bullet journal. And would it make the bib box unnecessarily chunky and uneven, as the stickers would add extra bulk? I suppose if you don't feel like doing it when you're making a new bibcard, don't do it, and embrace that sprinkling of chaos and imperfection that it's added to your notebox.

Either way it sounds fun. I like the idea of bibcards as a ritual when starting a new book and I think personalising them a little with an image could make the ritual even nicer. I have a thermal sticker printer that I want to use for this: Kathleen mentions that her printouts are about 2 inches tall, so I will print the cover 'sideways' using 57mm sticker paper to make each image 57mm tall.

  1. Although Kathleen credits someone else for the idea, I can't find an example of it on their channel. Maybe they've deleted the video? ↩︎
