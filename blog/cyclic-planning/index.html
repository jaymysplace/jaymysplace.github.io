This is my spin on the system shown in this video by JashiiCorrin. I've had a go at explaining the system in my own words here

I like to call the 4 'Reset Weeks' Week X, and add them into the last month of each quarter. So each month has Weeks 1-4, but March, June, September and December also have a Week X at the end. Weeks that don't belong to a particular month don't seem right to me! And I keep them abstracted from the usual Weeks 1-4 by giving them a non-numerical name.

I try to get time off work so that I can treat these X weeks as special. I'll be easier on myself in terms of habit tracking, and try and use the week as a time to rest, reflect and look ahead to the next quarter.

2026 is a 'leap year' for the cyclic calendar, so I'm having a 'Week XX' at the end of December. This is that week between Christmas and New Year when time isn't real anyway so it'll be fine!
