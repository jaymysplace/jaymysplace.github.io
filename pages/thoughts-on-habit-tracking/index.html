I'm thinking of moving from keeping a habit tracker as part of my Monthly Log page and instead just building it into my daily log. At the moment on the Monthly Log I have a grid representing the month, and I mark up one square depending on what happened that day. I'm not too sure that I need have all the squares in one place though?

I think what matters is what you're intending to do with the information your habit tracker provides. For me at the moment, I just want a little bit of an opportunity to say 'well done'to myself at the end of the day, so I think incorporating that little marking-a-box ritual into writing my Daily Log itself makes more sense: I'll already be looking at it to review the day and maybe write my 'homework for life’ I'm already there summarising my day so I may as well just stay there.

I like to change what I'm tracking fairly regularly and I think moving to 'daily tracking' actually makes this easier. I'm going to make a new collection called Tracker Definitions or something like that, and keep a log here whenever I change what I'm tracking. It will be dated, so it'll be easy to cross-reference with the daily logs.

On the other hand, if you're REALLY looking to spot patterns and trends, you might want more data than a monthly tracker on a single page could hold, and you might want to be able to crunch that data more than Is possible with a static grid on a page. That is to say you might want to be transferring your data into Excel or something, and if you're doings that, it doesn't really matter how it's presented in your notebook. Or heck maybe it would be easier to keep it entirely in app (and out of the notebook) at this stage?
