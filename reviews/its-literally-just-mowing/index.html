I got a free month of Apple Arcade recently and this is one of the games I tried during that time.

You can tell it's really one of those 'wait a long time or give us money' games, but that's been short circuited by it's move to Apple Arcade and presumably the boost to the amount of currency it gives you.

The game is called 'It's Literally Just Mowing' and that's accurate. Each level consists of a 'street', and each street has four areas of grass to mow. These can be the garden outside a house, or a larger area of grass that's part of a park or sports field. Clearing each area gives you some money (I can't remember what the currency is actually called), and clearing a whole street gives you even more. You'll also be given a loot box with a timer, and the option to spend currency to open the box right now. This is where the Apple Arcade boost makes the big difference, I think, because you're given more money than you'd ever need to open every box immediately. The loot boxes are nice, each one giving you a little (cosmetic-only) new part for your mower.

The actual mowing though! There's something about how the grass is animated, how the noise from the mower changes when you're pushing through all the long grass, the nice neat lines that you make. It is very satisfying. Considering the title of game tells you it's all mowing, the mowing is good. Of course, it gets repetitive, but is that really surprising?

There's a little extra challenge dotted around, as in each level you'll see birds flying around, or pets chilling outside houses. Clicking on these adds that animal to a little 'encyclopaedia', and gives you a little bit of money too, so that's one thing that brings a little bit more to the game while you're mowing.

At the time I played, there was also a summer event on. This just scattered sunflowers around each level and you had to tap on them to collect them. Then once you had ten or so you unlocked a new summer-themed part for your mower. It's something, but nothing too exciting.

If you have Apple Arcade, I think it's well worth playing a few levels. I don't think it's worth keeping around though. Enjoy the mowing until it feels repetitive, then stop.
