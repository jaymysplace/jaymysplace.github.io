Another game that I played during my free month of Apple Arcade. This one was the opposite of It's Literally Just Mowing.

I liked the calmness and orderliness of it all. Unpacking boxes after moving in real life is very stressful but here there's this nice feeling of just finding a place for everything. I loved the little satisfying click when you set something down. Story-wise: just lovely, very little said in words but all in the context of what you're unboxing and what your new place is like. I finished the last level and came away with this feeling of hope and optimism for my own life.

If every game on Apple Arcade stuck closer to this type of game, I would be very happily paying the monthly subscription.
